NYC property violations lookup

Open HPD, DOB, ECB and OATH records for any address in the five boroughs, pulled live from NYC Open Data.

A borough helps. A BBL such as 2-04853-0022 skips the address lookup entirely.

Results appear as they finish. Expect roughly two seconds per address.

Sources: HPD violations (wvxf-dwi5), DOB violations (3h2n-5cm9), DOB ECB violations (6bgk-3dad), OATH hearings (jz4z-kudi), HPD registrations (tesw-yqqr). Addresses are resolved to a borough, block and lot through NYC GeoSearch.

OATH covers every city agency that issues summonses, so a high OATH count is often sanitation or parking rather than building condition. The DOB-related figure is broken out separately. Open counts on OATH are approximate: a case is treated as closed when all terms have been met.
